Nierika is a modern alternative gallery app built with Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3 Expressive, focused on privacy, fluid performance, and powerful local media management.

Key Features:

• Material 3 Expressive & Large Screen Ready
Adaptive UI designed for foldables, tablets, and wide screens. Fluid spatial motion, dynamic wallpaper-based tonal color schemes, 13 handcrafted color palettes, and 8 customizable local fonts (including Google Sans Flex, Outfit, DM Sans, and Nunito).

• Custom Albums & Organization
Create custom collections with personalized covers, detailed descriptions, custom tags, and precise chronological sorting. Manage media in batches with ease.

• ZIP Export & Import
Seamlessly backup, export, or import entire photo albums in standard .ZIP archives directly on your device.

• Photo & Video Editor
- Canvas Expansion: Expand photo canvas to standard and custom aspect ratios with blurred or solid background fills.
- Fine Adjustments: Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, highlights, shadows, and warmth.
- Markup & Drawing: Freehand brush, pixelate (blur/censor tool), eraser, and rich text overlays.
- Video Editing: Trim video clips, adjust audio levels, mute sound, and extract high-resolution frames as photos. Powered by AndroidX Media3.

• Hidden Vault & App Disguise
- Secure Vault: A private, isolated sandbox locked with PIN and biometric authentication to keep sensitive photos and videos hidden from all other apps.
- Launcher Disguise: Change Nierika's launcher icon and app name on the fly to camouflage it as Nepo Calculator, Drafto Notes, Camera, or Library.

• EXIF Details & Privacy Cleaner
Inspect complete capture parameters (camera model, exposure, ISO, focal length, location altitude) and strip all metadata with a single tap before sharing.

• 100% Local & Offline Privacy
Zero tracking, zero analytics, and zero cloud uploads. Your memories stay strictly on your device.
